## Known issues
- **Twist metric is degenerate.** "No-twist" days are days 1-2 (probation, gentle), so their failed share is 0% (instant players) or 7.5% (`human`). Twist days fail 11-34% (greedy), 7-22% (careful), 27-48% (`human`). No twist is a death sentence, but the ≤ 2× rule can't be met against a 0% base. Needs a better yardstick (compare twist days with *non-twist days of the same day number*).
- **Careers still end on `sloppy`** (82-98% of failed shifts). The QA desk is the whole game; jams, overclocks and misroutes barely end careers. Greedy complaints (3.3/day) are high because greedy never scans.
- Careful beats greedy by 6 days (target 2): the gap may now be too wide for a human to feel in either direction.
- `human` plays at 7 days; offers are rarely taken (18-22% for `human`), so they matter little to it.
- Issue #67 is still open though its PR merged (#69).
- Headless `ui_check` prints a benign "1 ObjectDB instance leaked".
- DECISIONS #26 / `GAME_DESIGN.md` § M2 Screen still lag the two-screen layout (Jason's call).
